# Mascot avatars

Scraped from [ipaslogo.com](https://ipaslogo.com/) — free mascot logos made
with the [IP as Logo skill](https://github.com/s1dashu/ip-as-logo-skill).

Files under `webp/` are the site's 512px display renditions
(`cdn.ipaslogo.com/display-512/<name>.webp`). `manifest.json` maps each
file to its display name and the logo's background color.
