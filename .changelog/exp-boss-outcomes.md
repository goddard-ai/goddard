- **Boss outcomes and assignments:** the Boss can now coordinate durable outcomes — the result a unit of work should achieve — through bounded employee assignments. Ordinary successful assignments leave follow-up handoffs for the boss to resolve; a designated finishing assignment completes its outcome silently, without a Boss message or wake. Failures, blockers, and unfinished work still surface as needing attention. An open outcome with no active work and no waiting reason earns one batched reminder in Boss chat after 15 minutes, then at most once a day. Each outcome keeps a durable history of its assignments — who ran each attempt, its job, and how it ended — that survives employee retirement and app restarts; attempts recorded before the history existed are labeled unavailable rather than filled in with guesses. In Boss chat, the right panel's Tasks tab lists one expandable row per outcome — Pending, In progress, or Finished follows the outcome's own state — and expanding a row shows who is working or queued, any recorded wait or owed follow-up, and every earlier attempt in order, each opening its own conversation. Assignments without an outcome no longer appear there. The tab also opens itself at a compact width when work appears.
