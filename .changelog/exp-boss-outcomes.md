- **Boss outcomes and assignments:** the Boss can now coordinate durable outcomes — the result a unit of work should achieve — through bounded employee assignments. Ordinary successful assignments leave follow-up handoffs for the boss to resolve; a designated finishing assignment completes its outcome silently, without a Boss message or wake. Failures, blockers, and unfinished work still surface as needing attention. An open outcome with no active work and no waiting reason earns one batched reminder in Boss chat after 15 minutes, then at most once a day. In Boss chat, the right panel's Tasks tab also opens itself at a compact width when work appears there.
