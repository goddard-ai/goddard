- Plans now carry an ordered work breakdown the Goals panel groups on: finalization seeds the list from the approved document's course of work, and the Boss reshapes it through new `updatePlanItems`, `setPlanItemState`, and `setPlanOutcome` operations. Completed and abandoned plans record audited outcomes (who, when) and reopen to approved; done and dropped items reopen to to-do. `summon` and employee `control`'s new `setPlan` tag assignments to a plan and work item — unknown or finished references fail rather than landing untagged — and `BossState::plan_groups` exposes the plan-grouped, activity-ordered view the panel renders.
