- **VoicePad:** Hold Alt+Space to dictate into a chat's VoicePad without opening it; text lands when you let go. The latest recording shows as an always-editable bubble above the composer — typing elsewhere or clicking away hides it, Enter saves and returns to the composer, ⌘Z removes just that recording — and a "Voicepad · N lines" pill above the composer input surfaces accumulated text with one-click clear and an explicit undo. Clicking the pill reveals the latest transcription bubble without opening the VoicePad. A Whistle transcription engine is also selectable under VoicePad settings — AI Gateway stays the default. Enable both under Settings > Experiments > VoicePad. Whistle recording in the composer and VoicePad now continues until you stop, release the keys, or mute; the full transcript appears afterward, and failed transcription can retry the saved audio. With VoicePad open, its hint shows the hold shortcut; the control displays Recording during a hold and Unmute after release, and Unmute switches to continuous dictation.
