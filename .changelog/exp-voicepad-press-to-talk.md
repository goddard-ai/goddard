- **VoicePad:** Hold Alt+Space to dictate into a chat's VoicePad without opening it; text lands when you let go. The latest recording shows as an editable bubble above the composer — ⌘Z removes just that recording — and a "Voicepad · N lines" pill beside the composer surfaces accumulated text with one-click clear and an explicit undo. A Whistle transcription engine is also selectable under VoicePad settings — AI Gateway stays the default. Enable both under Settings > Experiments > VoicePad.
