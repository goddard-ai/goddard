- Summons no longer fail when model capacity is full: a valid summon is admitted as a durable queued ticket and dispatched in per-boss FIFO order as slots free. Boss-configured `setResourcePolicy` limits now cap each provider+model pair (`liveLimit`, with a `hardCap` reachable only by summons that explicitly ask for burst), and dispatch takes model and host resources atomically through the host broker, so queued employees hold no worktree, runtime, or device claims until they start. Prompts sent to a queued or finished employee park on its ticket and replay when it dispatches.
