- **Boss chains:** Paused chains gained owner recovery paths — a new `accept` control marks the current step's sealed handoff accepted despite a failed gate (explicit `resume` still advances), `revise` now works on the paused current step so a `retry` can dispatch an amended definition or raised `maxRetries`, and `retry` no longer needs `sourceRevision` when the attempt already recorded its reconciled base. Retries also stop burning budget on attempts that produced no structured result, `boss chain` prints a compact acknowledgement instead of the full state, and step prompts now tell employees the exact required check/artifact names to report under. Resume also checks authority before inspecting a handoff, accepts repaired gates without a stale-revision error, and concurrent admission cannot start duplicate employees.
