- ⌘T — or "New terminal in…" in the command palette — opens a terminal as a floating card at the top of the screen, focused and sized so the prompt is always visible, over whatever page you were on. A full-screen terminal's minimize button floats it and returns to the previous page; Escape or the card's close button hides the card while the shell keeps running, and ⌘W while it is focused kills the terminal.
