- In the VoicePad scratchpad, deleting a transcript selection now leaves a live caret — typing continues right where the cut happened, space included, and Backspace/Delete cut beside it. Enter always sends the scratchpad while it's up — a Mute pill click or a focused row no longer eats it — and drag-selecting with an empty annotation box open cancels the box so the selection can edit, while a box holding dictated text keeps the gesture from selecting.
