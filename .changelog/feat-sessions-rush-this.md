- Turn on “Rush this” from a sidebar task’s right-click menu or choose “Rush current task” in the command palette to enable fast service where available, briefly interrupting and resuming active work. A pulsing lightning icon on the sidebar task marks rush mode; input requests and finished tasks open the task’s usual conversation and controls in a modal when another task is selected. Queued prompts and active goals continue without opening the modal. Click outside or press Escape to dismiss without responding.
