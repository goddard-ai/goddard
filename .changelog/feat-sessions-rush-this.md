- Turn on “Rush this” from a sidebar task’s right-click menu or choose “Rush current task” in the command palette to enable fast service where available, briefly interrupting and resuming active work. Rushed tasks appear in a dedicated sidebar group above Pinned. Input requests and finished tasks open the task’s usual conversation and controls in a modal when another task is selected. Queued prompts and active goals continue without opening the modal. Click outside or press Escape to dismiss without responding. The modal keeps its width when the right panel is open behind it.
