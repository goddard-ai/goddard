- A VP mic button beside send in the composer starts a voice scratchpad: speak and a live transcript builds paragraph by paragraph in place of the chat, with "okay next" starting a new paragraph and clicking a paragraph or bullet capturing bullet annotations under it. The transcript is editable in place — select text and type to replace it or Delete to cut it, and arrows or a Tab landing walk a caret through paragraphs and bullets. Each chat keeps its own scratchpad: switching chats pauses the one you left and the VP button opens the chat you're viewing, where unmuting resumes it. Mute — or ⌥M while the scratchpad is up — pauses capture, Hide keeps dictating in the background of the same chat, and Enter sends the whole transcript as one message. Dictation streams the microphone to the `microsoft/mai-transcribe-2-streaming` model through the AI gateway, so it needs a gateway credential and microphone permission.
