- A VP mic button beside send in the composer starts a voice scratchpad: speak and a live transcript builds paragraph by paragraph in place of the chat, with "okay next" starting a new paragraph and clicking a paragraph or bullet opening an annotation box whose dictation lands as bullets under it — clicking elsewhere commits the open annotation. The transcript is editable in place — drag-select text and type to replace it or Delete to cut it, and arrows or a Tab landing walk a caret through paragraphs and bullets. Each chat keeps its own scratchpad: switching chats pauses the one you left and the VP button opens the chat you're viewing, where unmuting resumes it — clicking VP while the scratchpad is up cancels the session. Mute — or ⌥M while the scratchpad is up — turns off the microphone and closes the transcription stream until unmute reconnects, landing any half-dictated phrase as solid text; Hide keeps dictating in the background of the same chat, and Enter sends the whole transcript as one message. Dictation streams the microphone to the `microsoft/mai-transcribe-2-streaming` model through the AI gateway, so it needs a gateway credential and microphone permission. If the stream drops or the service pauses transcription mid-session, the scratchpad reconnects on its own and keeps the transcript. Left silent for four minutes a pad stops on its own — muted, transcript kept — and unmuting resumes it.
