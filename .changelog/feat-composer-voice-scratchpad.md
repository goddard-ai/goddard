- A VS mic button beside send in the composer starts a voice scratchpad: speak and a live transcript builds paragraph by paragraph in place of the chat, with "okay next" starting a new paragraph and clicking a paragraph capturing bullet annotations under it. Mute pauses capture, Hide keeps dictating in the background, and Enter sends the whole transcript as one message. Dictation streams the microphone to the `microsoft/mai-transcribe-2-streaming` model through the AI gateway, so it needs a gateway credential and microphone permission.
